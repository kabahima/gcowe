WHO WE ARE

Welcomes all people from different places regardless of their race, religious background, economic status and past failures. Our vision and mission is to be the streams of God’s Blessings as we reach the nations with the message of God’s love and Hope.

Founded on biblical principles, we strive to make a positive impact in our community through various ministries, outreach programs, and spiritual development opportunities.
