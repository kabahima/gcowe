Who we Are ?

Welcome to God's Center Of Blessings Church

Welcomes all people from different places regardless of their race, religious background, economic status and past failures. Our vision and mission is to be the streams of God’s Blessings as we reach the nations with the message of God’s love and Hope. Our role is to create an environment where people feel free to exercise their faith in God. Our deepest desire is to honor God and exalt Him, praise and worship is essential in pursuing a closer relationship with our Lord Jesus Christ, swinging the wider gates with thanks giving and His courts with praise, that creates an atmosphere to welcome the spirit of God to show up in a way that is explosive and able to transform our lives. The high energy of praise and intensity of worship prepares the way for the release of the relevant word of God.
